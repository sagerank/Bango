# Code of Conduct

Bango is a small project built by people who like their Macs. We want contributing to it to be pleasant for everyone, whatever their experience, background or hardware.

## Our standard

Expected:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people filing their first issue or pull request
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant
- Accept that a tap detector tuned for one Mac may behave differently on another, and report that with data instead of blame

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or repeated off-topic demands
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (Security tab, Report a vulnerability) and start the report with "Conduct", or contact the maintainer through the profile at https://github.com/sagerank. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
